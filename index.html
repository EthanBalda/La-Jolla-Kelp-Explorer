04 / Behind the experience

From measurements to meaning.

The research question

What relationship exists between ocean temperature anomalies and kelp canopy coverage near La Jolla, and how does regional upwelling fit into that picture?

This first version uses reproducible synthetic values to demonstrate the experience. It does not reproduce La Jolla’s history. The underwater scene is a visual metaphor, not a geographical map or ecosystem simulation.
